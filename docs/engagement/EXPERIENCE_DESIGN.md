# Educational experience design

## Product posture

RA / RealidadeA / ABC do Autista is an educational alphabet experience with sensory accessibility controls. It is predictable, repeatable and locally controlled. It makes no clinical or therapeutic claim.

There are no scores, ranks, lives, streaks, timers, punishment, advertising, continuous music, flashing effects, sudden camera motion, face recognition, microphone use or personal-data capture.

## Stable interface

- Upper left: current letter and word.
- Upper right: labelled Exit control.
- Lower left: labelled Replay narration control.
- Lower right: labelled interaction prompt/action.
- Lower centre: caption region.
- Step indicator: Listen, Discover, Interact, using text in addition to colour.
- Controls respect the safe area, maintain large touch targets and keep the physical target visible.

## Sensory contract

Calm is the default: captions on, ambient music off, restrained effects, one motion at a time and optional Reduced Motion. Balanced allows automatic narration and moderate motion. Expressive enriches motion and effects while retaining the same no-flash/no-aggressive-audio ceiling.

The learner can repeat, pause through target loss, leave, or try again without penalty. A completed experience does not restart in an infinite loop.
